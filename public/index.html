Why Roots & Wings

A living bridge between graduates and the students who'll follow them

Every university has decades of alumni scattered across companies and industries — an enormous, mostly untapped resource. Roots & Wings gives students a direct line to that experience, and gives alumni an easy, low-friction way to give back.

  • 01
    Verified alumni directory

    Search by university, company, industry, or skills.

  • 02
    Structured mentorship

    Request, accept, and schedule mentorship in a few clicks.

  • 03
    Jobs & internships pipeline

    Alumni post real openings; students apply and track status.

Platform Features

Everything both sides need, nothing they don't

Built for students chasing their first break and alumni who want mentoring to feel effortless.

Alumni Directory
